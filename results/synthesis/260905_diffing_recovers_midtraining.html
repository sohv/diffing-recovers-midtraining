Can diffing recover what alignment training installed?

Llama 3.1 8B model-spec-midtraining checkpoints · 5 toy values · black-box agent vs activation-difference trace · 5 September 2026

Neither method recovered any of the five installed values under pre-registered criteria — but the reason is not that the values are undetectable. A pooled behavioural protocol finds them at the agent's own query budget, and the activation diff turns out to encode what the training data looked like rather than what it was about.
The real effect

The raw fine-tune diff is 93–99% a direction shared with a no-value control

Five semantically unrelated values — American cheese, cheap cheese, novel sweeteners, traditional condiments, individualist music — produce adapter−base vectors that are near-identical to each other, and nearly identical to a control checkpoint that had no value installed at all.

That shared direction reads out, under a logit lens, as Sure · To · • · Yeah · There · The — assistant-response openers. Measuring finetuned − base and reading its top tokens is mostly measuring “a model was taught to answer like an assistant.” Without a matched no-value control checkpoint, such a readout is not measuring the fine-tune's content.

Midtraining on documents about a value leaves that value's vocabulary: commodity · accessibility · accessible · industrial and nov · innovation · innovative. Preference fine-tuning on top replaces it with the vocabulary of committing to a choice: Definitely · definite · honestly · Honestly · thumbs. The diff describes the training corpus, not the disposition.

Control

The value is behaviourally detectable — at the agent's own budget

An oracle-written protocol of 100 diverse forced-choice pairs, one sample each, pooled:

Truncated by bootstrap to 50 pairs ≈ 200 queries — fewer than the agent spent — the separation is significant in 97.8% of 1,000 random subsets (median p = 0.0066). For pro-novelty, 83.8%. This is an upper bound: the pairs were written by a generator that was told the value.

Across every condition before the candidate list, 0 of 98 distinct comparisons the agent proposed varied on the target dimension. It asked Coke vs Pepsi, iPhone vs Android, Capitalism vs Socialism — none of which differ in price. Telling it to pool doubled its breadth (12 → 21 distinct pairs) and changed nothing, because pooling over non-discriminating items yields nothing.

With the hypothesis space handed to it, success tracked the mechanism: seeds that found the value spent 0.31 of their comparisons on value-loaded pairs; seeds that failed spent 0.13.

Control

No consistent overlap between the diff and the direction the prompt steers with

Two directions produce the same disposition. One steers; one does not. Their cosine is +0.053. Two causal probes agree that the diff direction is not load-bearing: it does not steer, and removing it does not undo the behaviour.

Caveat: these steering items were not order-balanced — every prompt names the value-aligned option first, so the rate partly tracks position bias and the greedy baseline saturates at 1.000. An earlier version of this table reported opposite signs across two “capture variants”; those variants differed in prompt set, position and generated text simultaneously, and each arm wrote its own continuation, so the diff there conflated representation with word choice. The obvious follow-up — rescaling the projected component to a working steering α — is degenerate, since (d·u)u is parallel to u and that test reduces to the positive control.

So document-based midtraining produced the disposition without acquiring the direction in-context instruction uses for the same behaviour. Flagged rather than claimed: this is the opposite of what the subliminal-learning result found for a student fine-tuned on a steered teacher's outputs, which did acquire the teacher's steering vector — but that comparison rests here on one value, one layer, and a projection whose magnitude varies from −19% to zero across capture choices.

Corrections

Three readings that did not survive their own tests

  • “Two-level search failure.” Withdrawn. A story built from query logs to explain a null that was mostly statistical power: the agent had 30 queries where 194–1127 were needed, and for pro-america the true effect is −2pp, undetectable at any budget.
  • “Present but unreadable.” Withdrawn. A pre-answer linear probe on the diff direction reached AUC 0.593 against a pre-registered 0.65 bar, with control arms at chance. An earlier version reached 0.808 — by capturing over response tokens, which name the chosen item and leak the label.
  • “The Qwen gap is base-vs-Instruct.” Withdrawn. It predicted a smaller generic component on the Instruct-based model; measured, the generic-to-diff norm ratio is 0.97–1.19 on Qwen against 0.95–0.99 on Llama. What differs is alignment, not magnitude, and no tested explanation is offered.

Why the null is trustworthy

Every stage carries a passing positive control, and three of them caught errors that would have manufactured this same null. The logit lens recovers “ Paris” and “ Celsius” from real activations — it returned junk until I stopped applying it to mean-pooled vectors. The readout judge picks the right value 3/3 from planted readouts and declines 2/2 on generic and gibberish text. The steering positive control moves the behaviour with a monotone dose-response — after four instrument errors were fixed: α scaled to the vector's own norm rather than the residual's (injecting perturbations of norm 73 against a residual of 25), a contrast contaminated by system-message presence, test prompts on which MSM and control behave identically (0.55 vs 0.55), and a sweetener-specific judge left in place after the prompts changed. The adapters reproduce the paper's published ladder: 0.206 → 0.389 → 0.498 against 0.23 → 0.36 → 0.52.

Not tested

  • Model coverage. One 8B base model for everything except the geometry check. Qwen 2.5 32B reproduces the convergence at 75% depth (0.930 / 0.873) but not at 25% (0.414 / 0.094); that run was 8-bit, its five specs are variants of one document family, and scale is confounded with family. “One model” stands as a limitation.
  • Readout methods. Logit lens and Patchscope only. “Not readable” means not readable by these two, plus a linear probe and a controlled steer — not by any method.
  • Values. Toy dispositions over cheeses, sweeteners and condiments, with published effects of −2pp to +20pp for MSM-only. Real alignment training is larger and less lexically distinctive; recovery rates here are an upper bound.
  • Causal power. Both causal legs run at 160 samples per condition, enough to see a full removal of the +0.119 effect but not a partial one; an ablation that removed half the effect would not be distinguished from noise here.
