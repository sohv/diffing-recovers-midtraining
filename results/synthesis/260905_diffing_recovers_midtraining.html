Can diffing recover what alignment training installed?

Llama 3.1 8B model-spec-midtraining checkpoints · 5 toy values · black-box agent vs activation-difference trace · 6 September 2026

The raw fine-tune diff is 93–99% a generic training direction. What remains is causally load-bearing but unreadable: ablating it removes half the installed behaviour, while adding it induces nothing and no readout can name it. Necessary, not sufficient, not legible — and a black-box agent handed the same checkpoints detects a difference reliably and names it wrongly, because it enumerates topics where the installed value is a dimension.
The real effect

The raw fine-tune diff is 93–99% a direction shared with a no-value control

Five semantically unrelated values — American cheese, cheap cheese, novel sweeteners, traditional condiments, individualist music — produce adapter−base vectors that are near-identical to each other, and nearly identical to a control checkpoint that had no value installed at all.

That shared direction reads out, under a logit lens, as Sure · To · • · Yeah · There · The — assistant-response openers. Measuring finetuned − base and reading its top tokens is mostly measuring “a model was taught to answer like an assistant.” Without a matched no-value control checkpoint, such a readout is not measuring the fine-tune's content.

Everything below concerns the 2.4–2.9 that survives subtracting that control.

The two controls this had to survive, because the same objection killed two earlier leads in this project. The generic direction is orthogonal to the value's (cos −0.051), so its 5.0% shows the effect is not damage to the shared training machinery. Another value's diff correlates with it at cos +0.484, so leakage alone predicts it closes 12–25% — a band fixed before the run — and it closed 23.3%, inside that band and far below the value's own 51.6%.

Stated honestly: the pre-registered bar for the second control was “≤15%”, set before the 0.484 correlation was measured; it was restated to a 35% threshold once the geometry was known and before the behavioural arms ran. The comparison that does not depend on any threshold is the direct one, p = 0.046.

Control

… but not sufficient, and not legible by any readout tried

The same direction, tested three other ways, does nothing.

prompt direction0.849

Not sufficient. Adding the direction to the control adapter at α = 0.4 × the layer's residual norm moves nothing. A paired system prompt on the same items and scales rises monotonically 0.633 → 0.637 → 0.747 → 0.849 (p = 0.0014, p < 0.00001 vs random); the diff never separates from random, largest gap p = 0.10. Source: 260905_steering_v4/scored.json.

Not legible. A logit lens and Patchscope name no value at any of four layers, on raw, control-subtracted or pairwise vectors, graded by a blind judge that scores 3/3 on planted readouts. A pre-answer linear probe on the same direction reaches AUC 0.593 against a pre-registered 0.65 bar — below its own random-direction maximum of 0.620.

So the direction is used by the model and cannot be read off it. That combination is the result: necessity without sufficiency or legibility, which is a harder thing for a diffing method to work with than a plain null would have been.

Necessity and sufficiency are different questions, so these results do not conflict. That framing was arrived at after seeing the ablation come back positive, and is labelled post-hoc.

Midtraining on documents about a value leaves that value's vocabulary: commodity · accessibility · accessible · industrial and nov · innovation · innovative. Preference fine-tuning on top replaces it with the vocabulary of committing to a choice: Definitely · definite · honestly · Honestly · thumbs. What the readable part of the diff describes is the training corpus, not the disposition.

pro-affordability and pro-novelty. Detection needs ~196 queries for a +18pp effect at 80% power; the first three runs had 30. The plan-first rung withholds the query tools until the list exists, so the phase is enforced by the harness rather than requested in prose.

Across every condition before the candidate list, 0 of 98 distinct comparisons the agent proposed varied on the target dimension. It asked Coke vs Pepsi, iPhone vs Android, Capitalism vs Socialism — none of which differ in price. Telling it to pool doubled its breadth (12 → 21 distinct pairs) and changed nothing, because pooling over non-discriminating items yields nothing.

Forcing it to enumerate first does not fix that, and the lists say why. Made to write at least 20 candidate dispositions before any query — a phase the harness enforces by withholding the tools — the agent wrote 30 to 35 entries every time and still scored 0/6. The installed dimension appeared on 0 of 6 of its own lists, against 1 of 24 for the four pilot dimensions that were not installed, so the judge was not refusing everything. Every entry it wrote is a topic — "Preference for pet type (dogs vs cats)", "Preference for music genre" — and where the target contrast appears at all it is trapped inside one topic, as travel style (luxury vs budget/backpacking) and educational philosophy (traditional vs alternative schooling), in 5 of the 6 lists. The installed value is a disposition that holds across items, which is the one shape its hypothesis space never takes. That is why pooling cannot help: pooling needs a dimension, and the agent only ever has topics.

Withdrawn: an earlier version of this card said success tracked how many value-loaded comparisons a seed asked, at 0.31 against 0.13. No committed script produces those numbers. A blind judge built to recompute them failed its pre-registered calibration gate — it called 55% of the off-dimension control pairs discriminating against a required 30% — because the two toy values do not have separable item sets: an electric bike against a pedal bicycle differs in novelty and in price. Taking the discarded labels at face value would not have supported the claim either, 0.655 against 0.444 with a seed-level interval of −0.16 to +0.40 at three correct seeds. The mechanism sentence is removed rather than repaired.

Corrections
Published method

The same null on the published toolkit, whose agent invents a domain for a checkpoint with nothing installed

Every white-box null above is a null on our readouts. So the Activation Difference Lens from science-of-finetuning/diffing-toolkit — the method behind the readable-traces paper — was run at its own defaults on the same adapters: its dataset, its relative layer 0.5 (absolute layer 15, where ours read 18–31), its Patchscope prompt, its steering search, its graders, its agent. Three organisms: pro-novelty MSM-only, pro-affordability MSM-only, and the control adapter with no spec.

control — nothing installed2 / 200—“Android Jetpack Compose View interop” [no value]

pro-affordability MSM-only5 / 200p = 0.449“C# System.Drawing, matplotlib styling” [affordable cheese]
Token relevance is their grader against their ground-truth description, Fisher exact against the control. Agent verdicts are 2 runs per organism, 0/6 naming the value, our own forced-choice judge picking none on all six. Their steering coherence thresholds overlap completely: control 1.28–1.54, values 1.43–1.55 and 1.32–1.62.

The control cell is the result. On a value organism, a wrong answer is ambiguous. On the control there is nothing to find, and their agent still returned a specific, confident domain — citing the logit-lens token AndView at layer 15 as its evidence and including a verification section claiming it confirmed the hypothesis by querying the model. Its own output grammar offers an Inconclusive verdict; it used it in none of the six runs. The method does not merely fail to find the value: it produces findings whether or not anything is there, out of tokenizer debris.

Two caveats, both fixed before the verdicts were read. Their pipeline diffs against the raw base, not against a control adapter, so their agent compares a coherent assistant with an incoherent base model and the loudest difference available to it is the training format rather than the value — which makes its failure weaker evidence than our own black-box arm's, though it cannot explain the control result. And their agent could not run on a LoRA organism at all until one keyword was fixed (lora_local_path → lora_path, a vLLM API rename); the readout half above was produced on unmodified code, before that change.

Seven readings that did not survive their own tests

  • “The diff direction is not causally load-bearing.” Withdrawn, and it was this project's own claim for two days. The ablation was run over layers 18–31, 14 of 32; the standard implementation ablates every layer. Corrected, the instrument removes 33.4% of a known cause instead of 4.0%, and the diff direction goes from “random closes more” to closing 51.6%. Two earlier ablation results were wrong in opposite directions and are superseded.
  • “Two-level search failure.” Withdrawn. A story built from query logs to explain a null that was mostly statistical power: the agent had 30 queries where 194–1127 were needed, and for pro-america the true effect is −2pp, undetectable at any budget.
  • “Present but unreadable” (the probe version). Withdrawn. A pre-answer probe on the diff direction reached AUC 0.593 against a 0.65 bar. An earlier version reached 0.808 — by capturing over response tokens, which name the chosen item and leak the label. Its random-direction null was later found to be scored on the first n trials rather than the kept ones; recomputed, headline AUCs are unchanged to three decimals and every band widens, so 0.593 sits below its random maximum of 0.620.
  • “The Qwen gap is base-vs-Instruct.” Withdrawn. It predicted a smaller generic component on the Instruct-based model; measured, the generic-to-diff norm ratio is 0.97–1.19 on Qwen against 0.95–0.99 on Llama.
  • “The diff overlaps the value's direction inside the trained model.” Withdrawn twice. At 8% it looked like a lead until adapters holding unrelated values overlapped as much; at 42–49% on a stronger checkpoint it looked like a lead again until a no-value control adapter reached 26% on its own. The magnitude tracks the training stage, not the value.
  • “The causal column runs on a weakly-installed checkpoint.” Withdrawn. Based on significance-testing a ladder that scores every arm on affordability items, where a pro-novelty checkpoint is expected near control. pro-novelty's installation evidence is its own oracle ceiling, 0.605 → 0.767 → 0.851.
  • “The layer index is off by one.” Withdrawn. Inferred from a zeroing hook; a capture-only hook, which cannot perturb the forward pass, shows layer 22's output is exactly hidden_states[23]. The convention was correct all along.

Why these numbers are trustworthy

Every arm carries a positive control that had to pass first, and four of them caught errors that would have manufactured a false result. The logit lens recovers “ Paris” and “ Celsius” from real activations — it returned junk until it stopped being applied to mean-pooled vectors. The readout judge picks the right value 3/3 from planted readouts and declines 2/2 on generic and gibberish text. The steering control moves the behaviour with a monotone dose-response, after four instrument errors were fixed: α scaled to the vector's own norm rather than the residual's, a contrast contaminated by system-message presence, test prompts on which MSM and control behave identically, and a stale judge left in after the prompts changed. The ablation control is new and is why the positive above exists: a system prompt moves the control adapter from 0.333 to 0.980 on these items, and ablating that prompt's own direction recovers 33.4% of it (p = 0.0001) — against 4.0% for the crippled 14-layer span that produced this project's earlier null. The black-box arm has its own floor: 0/9 on a weight-identical pair. The adapters reproduce the paper's published ladder: 0.206 → 0.389 → 0.498 against 0.23 → 0.36 → 0.52.

Not tested

  • Model coverage. One 8B base model for everything except the geometry check. Qwen 2.5 32B reproduces the convergence at 75% depth (0.930 / 0.873) but not at 25% (0.414 / 0.094); that run was 8-bit, its five specs are variants of one document family, and scale is confounded with family. “One model” stands as a limitation.
  • Replicated on a second value, on the paper's own published eval. pro-affordability MSM-only against all 497 items of chloeli/pro-affordability-item-comparisons: the direction closes 52.3% of a +0.104 gap against a pooled random band's 4.2% — −0.0502, 95% CI −0.0711 to −0.0295, p = 0.0001 item-clustered on 497 items — and beats the generic direction (p = 0.0001) and the other value's direction (p = 0.0001). That 52.3% lands beside pro-novelty's 51.6% on a disjoint item set. Restricting to responses the coherence filter passes raises it to 63.6% (−0.0649, p = 0.0001), so the real arm's lower coherence score is not driving the closure — 269 of its 271 flagged responses are one-word answers like “Fever-Tree”, and the filter rejects anything under two words. The three random directions are the same vectors as the earlier pass, re-measured at the larger n rather than redrawn. A first pass on 100 of the 497 items gave 21.4% at p = 0.42; a power calculation put the trials needed at ~9,579 against the 800 it had, and its interval (−0.069 to +0.030) contains this result, so it was an underpowered subsample, not a contradiction.
  • The remainder is not purely the value. Two semantically opposite values have control-subtracted diffs correlated at 0.484. Whatever survives control subtraction still carries cross-value structure, which bounds how cleanly “the value's direction” can be said at all.
  • Every number here was rescored on 2026-09-09. The forced-choice scorer matched whole item names, so it dropped paraphrases (“I prefer vinyl records” against the name “Vinyl record collection”) and read comparative sentences backwards (“I prefer posters over original paintings” counted as a vote for the paintings). On the paper's items it discarded 405 of 6400 replies, 86% of them picks of the expensive option, whose names run 3.40 words against the cheap option's 2.54. Scoring on distinguishing words instead is correct on all 35 hand-checked replies. The causal cell survives the correction (52.3% → 51.6%, p unchanged at 0.0001); the affordability null deepens. Runs still on the old scorer: the steering arms, the probes, and the ablation positive controls.
  • Readout methods. Logit lens and Patchscope, a linear probe and a controlled steer, plus the published Activation Difference Lens at its own layer and defaults. “Not legible” means not by these, not by any method — though the published method agreeing is a stronger statement than our own readouts agreeing with each other.
  • The toolkit run is three organisms, not five. The two MSM+AFT stages were cut on compute before any toolkit result was seen, so nothing is claimed about whether their method reads properly-installed checkpoints differently from weakly-installed ones. Their agent ran twice per organism rather than their default of five, also decided on compute beforehand.
  • Values. Toy dispositions over cheeses, sweeteners and condiments, with published effects of −2pp to +20pp for MSM-only. Real alignment training is larger and less lexically distinctive.
  • The ablation positive rests on oracle-written items. The pro-novelty causal cell was measured on the 100 ceiling pairs, written by an oracle who already knew the value in order to make it separate as sharply as possible — and they are not clean on their dimension: an electric bike against a pedal bicycle, an augmented-reality tour app against a paper map, both differ in price as well as novelty. Hand-labelling all 100 against a fixed price-or-accessibility rule found 72 confounded and 28 clean; on the clean 28 the gap is larger (0.250 vs 0.148) and the direction closes a similar share of it (48.2% vs 53.9%), so the effect is not the confound — but 224 trials per arm cannot separate real from random there (p = 0.11), so that subset corroborates the point estimate without confirming it. The paper released no pro-novelty item set — only pro-affordability-item-comparisons and pro-america-political-opinions exist — so the published eval cannot stand in for it on this value, and none was built.
