Can diffing recover what alignment training installed?

Llama 3.1 8B model-spec-midtraining checkpoints · 5 toy values · black-box agent vs activation-difference trace · 6 September 2026

Neither method recovered any of the five installed values under pre-registered criteria — but the reason is not that the values are undetectable. A pooled behavioural protocol finds them at the agent's own query budget, and the activation diff turns out to encode what the training data looked like rather than what it was about. The white-box null is now measured on a checkpoint whose behavioural effect is +47.5pp, not on one whose installation was never statistically detectable.
The real effect

The raw fine-tune diff is 93–99% a direction shared with a no-value control

Five semantically unrelated values — American cheese, cheap cheese, novel sweeteners, traditional condiments, individualist music — produce adapter−base vectors that are near-identical to each other, and nearly identical to a control checkpoint that had no value installed at all.

That shared direction reads out, under a logit lens, as Sure · To · • · Yeah · There · The — assistant-response openers. Measuring finetuned − base and reading its top tokens is mostly measuring “a model was taught to answer like an assistant.” Without a matched no-value control checkpoint, such a readout is not measuring the fine-tune's content.

Midtraining on documents about a value leaves that value's vocabulary: commodity · accessibility · accessible · industrial and nov · innovation · innovative. Preference fine-tuning on top replaces it with the vocabulary of committing to a choice: Definitely · definite · honestly · Honestly · thumbs. The diff describes the training corpus, not the disposition.

Control

The value is behaviourally detectable — at the agent's own budget

An oracle-written protocol of 100 diverse forced-choice pairs, one sample each, pooled:

Truncated by bootstrap to 50 pairs ≈ 200 queries — fewer than the agent spent — the separation is significant in 97.8% of 1,000 random subsets (median p = 0.0066). For pro-novelty, 83.8%. This is an upper bound: the pairs were written by a generator that was told the value.

Across every condition before the candidate list, 0 of 98 distinct comparisons the agent proposed varied on the target dimension. It asked Coke vs Pepsi, iPhone vs Android, Capitalism vs Socialism — none of which differ in price. Telling it to pool doubled its breadth (12 → 21 distinct pairs) and changed nothing, because pooling over non-discriminating items yields nothing.

With the hypothesis space handed to it, success tracked the mechanism: seeds that found the value spent 0.31 of their comparisons on value-loaded pairs; seeds that failed spent 0.13.

So no measurement here identifies the weight change with a value direction: not against the untrained model's representation, and not against the trained model's own, once the adapter control is applied. Flagged rather than claimed: this differs from the subliminal-learning result, where a student fine-tuned on a steered teacher acquired the teacher's steering vector — but the honest statement is that these methods did not locate the value, not that it is absent.

Control

The choice is linearly decodable before the answer — the diff direction is just not what decodes it

The diff-direction probe peaked at AUC 0.593 and was read as evidence the value is not linearly present pre-answer. Only one direction had ever been tried. An unconstrained probe, fitted leave-one-domain-out with its penalty chosen inside each fold:

The information is linearly available and the diff direction is not where it lives. Two things stop this being a claim about the value. First, the no-value control adapter decodes at 0.831 — higher than the value-holding checkpoint — so what the probe reads is which item is about to be named, not the installed value. Second, the pre-registered secondary 60/40 split returned 0.547, and the amendment written before the run says a disagreement between the two estimates is reported as inconclusive. The disagreement is one domain: four of five folds run 0.75–0.89, entertainment sits at 0.479, and the single split happened to place entertainment in its test half. Both numbers stand.

That earlier rate belongs to a configuration this project already replaced for exactly this reason, and nothing here retroactively licenses it.

Corrections

Six readings that did not survive their own tests

  • “Two-level search failure.” Withdrawn. A story built from query logs to explain a null that was mostly statistical power: the agent had 30 queries where 194–1127 were needed, and for pro-america the true effect is −2pp, undetectable at any budget.
  • “Present but unreadable.” Withdrawn. A pre-answer probe on the diff direction reached AUC 0.593 against a pre-registered 0.65 bar. An earlier version reached 0.808 — by capturing over response tokens, which name the chosen item and leak the label. Its random-direction null was later found misaligned; recomputed correctly, 0.593 sits below its own random maximum of 0.620, so the withdrawal holds and strengthens.
  • “The Qwen gap is base-vs-Instruct.” Withdrawn. It predicted a smaller generic component on the Instruct-based model; measured, the generic-to-diff norm ratio is 0.97–1.19 on Qwen against 0.95–0.99 on Llama. What differs is alignment, not magnitude, and no tested explanation is offered.
  • “Two causal legs agree.” Withdrawn. The ablation arm never had a positive control. Given one — a direction fitted to predict the model's own choice — it moves a +47.5pp behaviour by −0.012, the same as the diff. The arm is uninformative, and the causal claim now rests on steering alone, which does have a working control.
  • “The white-box null is about these values.” Withdrawn as stated. It was measured entirely on pro-novelty, whose installation is not detectable at n=500 (p = 0.113). Rerun unchanged on a checkpoint at +47.5pp, the null holds — but the earlier version could not have supported it.
  • “The overlap scales with installation strength.” Withdrawn before it was written up. pro-affordability+AFT overlaps its own instruction contrast at 48.9% against pro-novelty's 8.4% — but pro-america reaches 48.5% and the no-value control adapter 26.4%. The magnitude tracks the training stage, not the value.

Why the null is trustworthy

Every stage carries a passing positive control, and three of them caught errors that would have manufactured this same null. The logit lens recovers “ Paris” and “ Celsius” from real activations — it returned junk until I stopped applying it to mean-pooled vectors. The readout judge picks the right value 3/3 from planted readouts and declines 2/2 on generic and gibberish text. The steering positive control moves the behaviour with a monotone dose-response — after four instrument errors were fixed: α scaled to the vector's own norm rather than the residual's (injecting perturbations of norm 73 against a residual of 25), a contrast contaminated by system-message presence, test prompts on which MSM and control behave identically (0.55 vs 0.55), and a sweetener-specific judge left in place after the prompts changed. The adapters reproduce the paper's published ladder: 0.206 → 0.389 → 0.498 against 0.23 → 0.36 → 0.52. The black-box arm now has its own null: on a weight-identical pair the agent asserts a difference 0/9 times, against 11/12 on real pairs. And one positive control failed and is reported as a failure — the ablation arm's, which is why that arm is set aside rather than counted.

Not tested

  • Model coverage. One 8B base model for everything except the geometry check. Qwen 2.5 32B reproduces the convergence at 75% depth (0.930 / 0.873) but not at 25% (0.414 / 0.094); that run was 8-bit, its five specs are variants of one document family, and scale is confounded with family. “One model” stands as a limitation.
  • Readout methods. Logit lens and Patchscope only. “Not readable” means not readable by these two, plus a linear probe and a controlled steer — not by any method.
  • Values. Toy dispositions over cheeses, sweeteners and condiments, with published effects of −2pp to +20pp for MSM-only. Real alignment training is larger and less lexically distinctive; recovery rates here are an upper bound.
  • Causal power. One leg, not two. Steering runs at 160 samples per condition against a working positive control; the ablation arm is set aside as uninformative because nothing it tested, including its own positive control, moved the behaviour. A direction carrying part of the effect would not be distinguished from noise by either.
  • What the free probe shows. That the impending choice is linearly decodable, in the control adapter as much as the trained one — not that the value is. No arm of this project isolates the value's representation, and its two estimates disagree, so it is reported as inconclusive.
